# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Fans looking for music, videos, merchandise, tour dates, and direct artist updates.
- Promoters, venue buyers, festival programmers, booking agents, press, journalists, brand partners, and collaborators evaluating JayDubb Tha Ruler.
- JayDubb and the site team maintaining releases, shows, press, Journal content, VIP signups, and booking inquiries.

## Product Purpose

The official JayDubb Tha Ruler platform presents the artist's catalog and story, converts fan interest into listening, ticket, merchandise, and VIP actions, and gives industry contacts a credible route to booking and press materials.

## Positioning

An artist-owned career platform that joins JayDubb's independent catalog, live history, The 7 brand, verified press, and direct booking infrastructure in one canonical destination.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive from social profiles, streaming services, shared show links, press outreach, and direct promoter communication. The site must work on mobile in distracted, one-handed contexts and on desktop when industry contacts need to copy biography text or download approved assets quickly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, native CSS, Supabase, and npm are established architecture.
- Supabase owns published show data. Authentication and schema are outside the current upgrade unless a concrete requirement emerges.
- Native browser APIs are preferred for observation, clipboard, and sharing.
- The site must retain its current URLs, analytics attributes, structured artist identity, booking workflow, and external The 7 integration.
- New work must avoid unnecessary dependencies, autoplay media, fake claims, and invented professional results.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: JayDubb Tha Ruler.
- Canonical artist identity: `https://jaydubbtharuler.com/#artist`.
- Origin: Colorado Springs, Colorado.
- Visual language: editorial independent hip-hop, tour collateral, artist archive, and controlled digital brutalism.
- Preserve the existing black, cream, red, and blue world; Anton display type; IBM Plex Mono metadata; Manrope body type; official wordmark; and existing photography.
- The 7 is an established artist brand and external merchandise destination. Do not redraw its marks or recreate the store in this sprint.

## Evidence on Hand

- Verified biography material in `JayDubb Tha Ruler Bio.txt` and the homepage Story section.
- Official photography and wordmarks under `public/images/`.
- Verified release, collaboration, live-support, press, video, and show content in the current repository and production site.
- No verified awards, audience totals, stream counts, ticket totals, testimonials, or performance metrics are available. Future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Preserve the artist identity before expanding capability.
2. Put the highest-value action within easy reach without obscuring the work.
3. Treat professional credibility as factual utility, not decorative marketing.
4. Prefer the existing system and native platform features over new technology.
5. Verify behavior in a real browser before calling an upgrade complete.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Public surfaces require semantic headings, keyboard access, visible focus, meaningful labels, practical touch targets, useful state feedback, safe-area support, and reduced-motion behavior.
